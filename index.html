Yes! We deliver to all governorates and major cities — Amman, Zarqa, Irbid, Aqaba, Madaba, Jerash, Ajloun, Karak, Ma'an, Tafilah and more. Delivery is fast and handled locally.
Are your products 100% original?
Absolutely. Every product we sell is 100% authentic and sourced from official brands. We never sell replicas or refurbished units as new.

We support Cash on Delivery as well as other convenient payment methods. You'll choose your preferred option at checkout, and our team confirms every order over WhatsApp.

Most orders are delivered within 24 hours inside Amman and 1–3 days to other cities, depending on your location. We'll keep you updated the whole way.
